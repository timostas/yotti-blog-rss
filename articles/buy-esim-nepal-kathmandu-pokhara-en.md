Install a Nepal eSIM before departure, then select it for mobile data after arrival. In Kathmandu it helps with accommodation addresses, transport and messages; in Pokhara it supports city navigation and contact with your host. The transfer between the two bases needs a separate offline layer. A ticket and arrival address should never exist only behind a live connection.

The Yotti Nepal page shows current packages, validity, hotspot support and the activation trigger. Check those details before purchase. This guide does not freeze a price or promise reception in every valley.

## Check the phone and plan before paying

First confirm that the device supports eSIM. Then make sure it is not locked to the home carrier. Finally, compare package validity with the whole journey, including arrival and departure days.

If another person or a laptop needs the connection, check hotspot rules explicitly. A larger allowance is not useful when the selected package cannot serve the intended device. On a Kathmandu–Pokhara trip, clear validity and a workable top-up path matter more than the biggest headline number.

Install over reliable Wi-Fi. Keep the order email and QR code somewhere separate, label the line “Nepal”, and do not delete it after setup. A second scan should not be assumed to restore the profile.

## Kathmandu: mobile data for precise addresses

In the capital, the connection mainly reduces friction between the airport, accommodation and the first neighbourhood. Save the hotel name, address and a landmark as text. A driver may recognise the place differently from the map, and similar names can occur in different parts of the city.

Keep the first walk within one area. Durbar Square, Asan and nearby courtyards can join gradually, but traffic and narrow lanes change the real pace. The map should help you return, not keep your attention on the screen throughout the walk.

Before leaving for Pokhara, save the ticket, departure point, operator contact and next accommodation address. Do not leave them only in an app that might request another login.

## Treat the transfer as a different mode

The road between Kathmandu and Pokhara should not rely on the same assumptions as city navigation. Download the corridor, capture the ticket and mark the arrival point before departure. Those details remain usable when the vehicle is delayed or the connection drops.

Download only what you will check: the two cities and the route between them. A vast regional map is harder to verify quickly. Save the accommodation number and the time after which a late arrival should be reported.

Do not make the road the first activation attempt. If setup needs Wi-Fi or support, it is easier to resolve at a Kathmandu hotel than during a transport stop.

## Pokhara: city navigation and a lakeside day

In Pokhara, distinguish Lakeside, the accommodation and the next departure point. They may look like one small zone on a zoomed-out map, but baggage, rain and an early start change the practical distance.

## If mobile data does not appear

After Airplane Mode is off, give the phone a few minutes. Confirm that the “Nepal” line is enabled and selected for mobile data. A travel profile may need data roaming; follow the instructions for the package you bought.

If the phone does not register, refresh the radio once with Airplane Mode and keep network selection automatic unless the provider says otherwise. If a network name appears but pages do not load, recheck the data line and any access-point settings from the order message.

Do not erase the eSIM to start over. Connect to Wi-Fi and send support the order number, phone model, displayed network name and settings screenshots without the QR code. That preserves the evidence needed to diagnose the failure.
