The Medina of Sfax is unusually legible for an old city. Its regular plan and principal axis let you spend less effort navigating and more attention on work in progress. Give the morning to one material, understand what the object does, then return to the scale of house, gate and wall.

## Enter with a direction, not a shopping list

UNESCO’s Tentative List description emphasises the orthogonal plan and the axis between the historic gates. Hold that direction lightly instead of collecting purchases. The approach makes shifts between trading lanes and working doorways easier to notice.

## Ask about function before price

With a metalworker, ask what a form is used for, how it is shaped and how it ages. With a woodworker, ask about species and household purpose. A purchase is optional; respect for the working doorway is not. Keep the entrance clear and ask before photographing a person.

## Let one courtyard change the scale

The city’s municipal heritage page describes Dar Jellouli as a house that presents traditional urban life. If access is confirmed, use it as an architectural pause. If it is closed, do not improvise facts—continue along the axis.

<figure class="yotti-photo"><img src="https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/sfax-courtyard.webp" srcset="https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/sfax-courtyard-384w.webp 384w, https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/sfax-courtyard-720w.webp 720w, https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/sfax-courtyard-960w.webp 960w, https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/sfax-courtyard.webp 1440w" sizes="(max-width: 760px) calc(100vw - 56px), 760px" width="1440" height="960" loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="A quiet courtyard in a traditional Sfax house"><figcaption>Domestic scale clarifies the environment for which craft objects were made.</figcaption></figure>

## Leave through the gate and stop

The wall brings the Medina’s geometry back into view. Compare its sequence with [Rabat](https://yotti.net/en/blog/morocco/one-day-in-rabat-medina-udayas-and-the-atlantic), then compare process-led observation with [saa paper in Chiang Mai](https://yotti.net/en/blog/thailand/a-craft-morning-in-chiang-mai-saa-paper) and [workshops in Kigali](https://yotti.net/en/blog/rwanda/a-craft-day-in-kigali-workshop-market-and-a-conversation-about-materials). The morning needs no distant add-on.
