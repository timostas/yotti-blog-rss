For a first week in Oman, two bases are enough. Spend three nights in Muscat, then move inland for three nights in Nizwa. The capital brings together the coast, souqs and museums; Nizwa provides a practical starting point for forts, old settlements and mountain landscapes. Changing hotel every day only makes the distances feel longer.

Oman can look compact on a route map. On the ground, heat, driving time and unplanned stops fill a day quickly. A pair of well-chosen addresses keeps the trip flexible when the weather or road changes.

## Three nights along Muscat’s coast

Muscat stretches through several districts rather than gathering around one walkable centre. Choose accommodation according to the places you plan to visit. For a first stay, mark Mutrah, the museum and palace area, the Grand Mosque and one section of coast before deciding where to sleep.

Use the first evening for Mutrah’s corniche and souq. It is an easy introduction after a flight: the harbour stays in view while the market brings incense, textiles and daily trade into the same walk. Tour the lanes before buying. Returning to a shop after comparing a few stalls is calmer than bargaining over the first object you notice.

Choose one cultural anchor for the next day. The National Museum, Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque and Royal Opera House each need real time. Opening hours, visitor access and clothing requirements belong to the official page of the venue, since the rules differ and can change.

Keep the third day for a coastal outing or a slower part of Muscat. The country’s official tourism portal presents the capital through seafront promenades, traditional souqs, forts, museums and nearby coastal areas. Select two or three of those threads rather than attempting the entire list in one visit.

## Treat the drive to Nizwa as a travel day

Plan the inland transfer during daylight and download the route before leaving. Save the accommodation address and useful stops offline. When collecting a rental car, document its condition and confirm that your agreement covers the roads you intend to use. Mountain access can carry separate vehicle requirements, which must be checked for the particular route.

Avoid stacking several forts onto the transfer. One stop is plenty on a first driving day. Arriving in Nizwa in time to check in and walk around the market is more rewarding than reaching town after every door has closed.

## Two full days in and around Nizwa

Start the first morning in Nizwa itself. The souq, fort and older lanes belong to the same day and do not require a long drive. Go before the hottest hours, checking current admission and opening times with the individual sites. Leave space for the market as a working place: dates, pottery and a short exchange with a seller add context that a rushed circuit of rooms cannot supply.

For the second day, choose one direction. Bahla appeals to travellers interested in fortifications and earthen architecture. Jebel Akhdar or Jebel Shams suits a day prepared around mountain roads and landscape. Experience Oman’s Al Dakhiliyah guide also covers historic settlements, wadis and outdoor activities across the region. Read it as a choice of possible days, not a checklist.

If cloud, heat or vehicle restrictions make the mountain plan unsuitable, substitute a museum, fort or unhurried Nizwa day. That change should be built into the itinerary before travel.

## A respectful pace works best

Clothing that covers shoulders and knees is practical in many public and cultural settings. Ask clearly before taking a close photograph of another person. A market seller should not become an uninvited portrait simply because the light is good.

Use the hottest middle hours for rest, an indoor visit or a transfer, then return to walking later. This pattern is kinder than forcing a northern-European sightseeing timetable onto the climate and leaves more attention for the next drive.

## Decide where to spend the final night

An early flight usually makes a final night near Muscat the safer choice. With a later departure, staying in Nizwa can work, but allow separate time for the drive, fuel, car return and airport procedures. Do not place an international flight directly after a mountain excursion.
