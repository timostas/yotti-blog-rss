An erased eSIM does not return from a normal phone backup. The carrier or travel-eSIM provider needs to make a profile available again. Re-scanning the old QR code may fail because activation details can be single-use, so connect to reliable Wi-Fi and open the original order before trying another installation.

Look for a reinstall or replacement option attached to that order. If none appears, contact support with the order number. Do not buy a second plan until the provider confirms that the existing package cannot be reissued.

## Check whether the line was erased or merely switched off

On iPhone, open Settings and Cellular or Mobile Data. On Android, inspect the SIM list under Network & internet, Connections or SIM manager. A disabled line remains listed and can be turned on again. An erased profile is absent.

Read every line label rather than relying on colour. Phones used for several trips often contain old profiles with names such as “Travel.” Match the destination and purchase date with the provider account. Keep full EID values and activation QR codes out of screenshots.

If the correct profile is still present, stop the recovery process and diagnose why that line is off or disconnected. Installing the same order over an existing profile can create a different error.

## The order can survive even when the profile does not

Deleting the phone’s profile and cancelling a plan are separate actions. Apple notes that erasing an eSIM does not itself cancel cellular service; cancellation still requires the carrier. A travel package may therefore remain active in the account even though the device no longer holds the credentials needed to use it.

The old QR code is not a general backup. Providers decide whether it can be downloaded again, reset or replaced. An “already used” response describes the activation code, not necessarily the remaining data.

Never substitute an SM-DP+ address or activation code found in a forum. Those details belong to another provisioning route and cannot retrieve your purchased profile.

## What to collect before contacting support

Open the account used for the purchase and record:

- the order number and destination;
- whether the plan still shows active data and validity;
- the approximate time of deletion;
- the phone model and current operating-system version;
- any exact message shown when the old installation method is tried once.

A cropped screenshot of the error is useful. The QR code, password, one-time codes and complete device identifiers are not.

For a [Yotti eSIM](https://yotti.net/en/), the current order is the key reference. Support needs to know whether the same phone is being restored or whether the service was moved to another handset.

## Recovery on iPhone

Apple advises against erasing an eSIM as a troubleshooting step unless the carrier directs it. Once it has been erased, Apple’s route is to contact the carrier for a new eSIM and set that up.

Do not factory-reset the iPhone or restore an old backup solely for this purpose. After the provider issues a supported installation method, open Cellular or Mobile Data, choose Add eSIM and follow those specific instructions.

If a transfer to a new iPhone happened just before the deletion, inspect the new phone first. The working line may already be there, and the old-device entry may have been removed as part of a completed move.

## Recovery on Android

Google separates turning a Pixel SIM off from Erase eSIM. Turning it off preserves the profile. Erasing removes it from the device, so the original line cannot simply be toggled back on.

After the provider authorises another download, Pixel users can start from Settings, Network & internet, SIMs and Add SIM. Samsung and other Android phones use different menu names, but the same boundary applies: only the provider can make valid credentials available again.

Avoid a full network reset. It cannot change a single-use code on the provider’s system and may remove saved Wi-Fi at the moment it is needed for installation.

## Interpret the provider’s answer precisely

Support may reactivate the old installation method, issue new activation details or replace the profile altogether. Treat these as separate outcomes. Reuse an old code only after explicit confirmation that it has been reset. Where new details are issued, archive the old message so the two sets are not confused.

If a replacement profile is required, ask whether the remaining allowance and validity move with it. A phone menu cannot show the commercial status of the old order. Where a new purchase is suggested, obtain a clear answer about the unused package before paying again.

After recovery, label the line with the destination and travel date. Pixel can require authentication before an eSIM is erased, which is useful on a device holding several similar profiles. During a future factory reset, read the separate choice about keeping or erasing cellular plans rather than assuming they follow ordinary backup data.

## Stay connected while the case is reviewed

Use trusted Wi-Fi or a companion’s hotspot. The home SIM can remain enabled for calls and account codes, but turning on its mobile data abroad may trigger roaming under the home carrier’s terms.

Do not erase other stored profiles to “make space” until the exact device limit has been checked. Once replacement details arrive, use them on stable Wi-Fi and confirm that the new line appears before changing data settings.
