An eSIM can provide a phone hotspot in the same way as a physical SIM, provided the handset supports tethering and the plan permits it. The important restriction usually belongs to the package rather than the SIM format. Some plans block hotspot use, apply a separate allowance or reduce speed.

Read the current terms of the selected [Yotti plan](https://yotti.net/en/) before relying on it for laptop work. If tethering is not mentioned, ask support for a direct answer.

## Prove that phone data works first

A hotspot cannot repair a travel line that has no data. Turn off Wi-Fi and load a webpage on the phone. Confirm that the travel eSIM is selected for mobile data and has the roaming setting required by the plan.

If the phone works but a connected laptop does not, the fault has narrowed to plan policy, hotspot configuration or the receiving device. There is no reason to change the mobile APN at random.

## Use the selected data line on iPhone

Apple uses the line chosen for Cellular Data for Personal Hotspot. Select the travel eSIM under Settings and Cellular, then open Personal Hotspot, allow connections and set a strong password.

If Personal Hotspot is missing, the carrier settings may not expose it or the plan may not support it. Restarting and checking for carrier-setting updates are reasonable, but the provider decides whether tethering is allowed.

## Set up hotspot on Android

Samsung places the controls under Connections and Mobile Hotspot and Tethering. Pixel uses Network & internet and Hotspot & tethering. Choose a network name, WPA2 or WPA3 security and a password, then turn the hotspot off when work is finished.

Connection limits and automatic shutdown can reduce unwanted use and battery drain. They are particularly useful when several companions know the password.

## The laptop connects but has no internet

Retest a webpage on the phone. Check remaining data and any separate tethering allowance. Samsung notes that devices can remain connected to a hotspot even when the permitted tethering data is exhausted.

Forget the network on the laptop and join again. Disable VPNs briefly on both devices. Use a special tethering APN only when the provider supplies one for this plan.

## Stop background tasks consuming the package

Mark the laptop connection as metered. Pause operating-system updates, cloud drives and backups. Video meetings and large transfers can consume far more than email or document editing.

Watch the provider's allowance display. The phone's counter may combine on-device and tethered traffic. When several people share the line, one person should own the hotspot and switch it off after use.
