A week of maps, messages, bookings and occasional photo uploads can fit within a few gigabytes. Streaming video, video calls, cloud backup and laptop tethering can multiply that figure. Estimate a travel eSIM from the activities that will happen away from Wi-Fi rather than relying on a fixed allowance per day.

Current package sizes and validity are listed on [Yotti](https://yotti.net/en/). Before choosing one, open your phone's mobile-data statistics and look at an ordinary week. Personal evidence is more useful than a universal consumption table.

## Establish a normal travel day

List what must work on cellular data: navigation, messaging, digital tickets, accommodation contact and local search. Taking a photo uses no data until the phone uploads or shares it. Offline maps and downloaded tickets reduce the baseline without changing the trip.

Reset the phone's data counter for one representative day at home. One traveller streams music for hours, another makes a daily video call, while someone else uses hotel Wi-Fi for everything beyond maps. Those differences dominate the estimate.

## Identify the heavy uses

Video streaming, video calls, original-quality photo backup, app updates and hotspot use consume data quickly. Navigation is often lighter than expected, particularly when the area map is downloaded first. Music varies with stream quality and listening time.

A tethered laptop can download an operating-system update, synchronise a cloud folder or start a backup without asking. Samsung notes that hotspot allowances and policies can differ from ordinary phone data, so check the plan rather than assuming every gigabyte can be shared.

## Match the package to the trip

A small allowance suits a short break built around maps, messages and bookings, with media waiting for Wi-Fi. A middle range fits daily social use, music and photo sharing. Regular video meetings, remote work or consistently poor Wi-Fi justify a larger plan.

“Unlimited” still needs interpretation. A plan may reduce speed after a daily threshold or restrict tethering. The label alone does not confirm that an evening work call will remain usable.

## Add one ordinary day of reserve

A practical buffer is another normal day, not twice the entire estimate. It covers a delayed flight or a hotel Wi-Fi failure without paying for an extreme scenario throughout the trip.

For several countries, confirm whether one regional plan covers the route. Separate profiles can reduce unused allowance but introduce more activation dates and line switching.

## Reduce waste before departure

Download maps, pause app updates and cloud photo backup on mobile data, and lower streaming quality. Both iPhone and Android allow cellular access to be disabled per app. Before tethering a laptop, mark the connection as metered and pause cloud synchronisation.

Use the provider account for remaining allowance as well as the phone counter; rounding can differ. If consumption jumps, per-app statistics usually reveal why.
