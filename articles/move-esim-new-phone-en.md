Moving an eSIM depends on the type of line and the provider's support. A home number may transfer through built-in iPhone, Pixel or Samsung tools. A prepaid travel profile is more likely to be tied to one device and may need fresh activation details or help from support. Keep the old profile until the new line has passed a real test.

Before changing phones, save the order number, support contact and terms for the [Yotti plan](https://yotti.net/en/). Check whether the profile can be transferred or downloaded again. Possessing the original QR code does not mean it is reusable.

## Identify the line before choosing a method

A regular carrier eSIM represents an ongoing phone number and account. Participating networks can support operating-system transfer. A travel eSIM is often a prepaid data package with a validity window and a device-specific installation rule.

There is little reason to move an expired package after the trip. Buy a new plan for the next journey. If the package is unused or active, obtain a provider decision before changing the old phone.

## Transfer from one iPhone to another

Apple offers eSIM Quick Transfer through supported carriers. Keep both iPhones nearby, unlocked, signed into the relevant Apple account and running suitable iOS versions. On the new device, open Settings, Cellular and Add eSIM, then choose transfer from another iPhone when offered.

The old line normally deactivates after the new one is activated. Test a call for a regular number or switch off Wi-Fi and test data for a travel profile. Do not erase the previous phone before this succeeds.

Where Quick Transfer is unavailable, the carrier may push a new profile or supply another QR code. Moving between Android and iPhone more often requires provider involvement.

## Transfer to a Google Pixel

Google supports moving a physical SIM or eSIM into an eSIM during new-phone setup or later through Network & internet, SIMs, Add SIM and Transfer SIM from another device. Carrier participation determines availability.

Automatic transfer requires compatible Android software, current Google Play services and a screen lock. If the option does not appear, contact the carrier. Google notes that the old device or profile may keep working after an unsuccessful attempt, which is another reason not to erase it early.

## Samsung and other Android phones

Some Galaxy devices offer Transfer SIM from another phone inside SIM manager. Other manufacturers use different routes. A visible transfer button still depends on carrier approval and can require identity verification or new activation details.

Cross-brand moves are more likely to need support than transfers within one platform. Have the new IMEI and EID ready, but share them only through an official channel.

## Test before clearing the old device

Confirm that the new profile is listed, registers on a network and performs its intended job. For data, disable Wi-Fi and load a webpage. For a phone number, test a call and message. Check access to banking and messaging services that may still depend on the old handset.

Only then remove the old profile and wipe the device. If transfer stops, record the error rather than repeatedly scanning the same code. Installation and dual-line guidance is organised under [Travel connectivity](https://yotti.net/en/blog/tag/Travel%20connectivity).
