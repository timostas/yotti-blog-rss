A carrier-locked phone generally cannot activate a travel eSIM from a different provider. The presence of eSIM hardware or an Add eSIM menu does not remove the lock. Confirm both device support and unlocked status before buying the plan.

The carrier or retailer that imposed the restriction controls its removal. An activation QR code, APN change or unofficial unlock website cannot override the account status held by that company.

## Establish where the phone came from

A handset bought unlocked at retail may already accept different networks. A subsidised or financed device bought from a mobile carrier can remain restricted until its contract or payment conditions are met. The receipt, carrier account and original sales terms are better evidence than the SIM tray.

If the seller is unknown, identify it from the purchase record or ask the current carrier. Do not spend a travel activation code merely to discover whether another network is allowed.

## iPhone has a visible lock field

Open Settings → General → About and find Network Provider Lock or Carrier Lock. Apple says an unlocked iPhone displays “No SIM restrictions.” That wording answers the lock question; the next checks concern eSIM capability and plan coverage.

Use the result as a gate:

- **No SIM restrictions:** proceed to exact-model compatibility and the provider’s installation method.
- **A restriction is shown:** contact the network provider that sold or locked the iPhone. Apple cannot unlock it on the carrier’s behalf.
- **The field is missing or unclear:** ask the original provider to confirm status using the IMEI through its secure channel. Do not erase the phone to create an answer.

Once the carrier confirms that the unlock has completed, follow its final instructions before adding the travel line. Keep that confirmation available until a different provider has passed a cellular-data test.

## Pixel uses the same rule, with a different check

Google states that a SIM-locked Pixel can receive mobile service only from the provider to which it is locked until the seller removes the restriction. Google Store phones are sold unlocked; phones bought through another carrier follow that seller’s terms.

There is no universal Android menu label covering every manufacturer and sales region. For a Pixel or another Android handset:

1. record the exact model and place of purchase;
2. check the seller’s account or contract for unlock status;
3. request confirmation from that carrier;
4. after completion, install current system updates and add the eSIM through the provider’s documented route.

Hidden dialler codes copied from an unrelated model do not provide portable proof. A factory reset is also a poor first test: it removes local data but does not cancel a carrier restriction stored in the seller’s systems.

## Place the activation failure correctly

### A different provider is rejected and the phone is locked

Stop the eSIM attempt. Resolve the restriction with the original carrier. The travel provider should not be asked for a workaround to another company’s device policy.

### The phone is unlocked but the QR code fails

Move to installation evidence: exact model, stable Wi‑Fi, order status and the complete error message. Do not continue treating carrier lock as the cause after it has been officially cleared.

### The profile installs but has no network

The download has passed. Check destination coverage, enabled line, package validity and the provider’s roaming instruction. Keep the profile installed while support reviews the network state.

### Unlock status cannot be confirmed before departure

Use a verified unlocked handset, trusted Wi‑Fi or a tested tethering arrangement. Paying an unknown site for a code creates a security and refund problem without reliable proof that the carrier record changed.

## Keep the two support conversations separate

The original carrier may require the IMEI, account holder details, device order and evidence that its unlock conditions have been satisfied. Submit those through its official account, app, shop or support route.

The travel eSIM provider needs the phone model, purchase market and confirmed unlocked status, followed by the order and activation error if one occurs. It does not need the financing agreement or password for the home-carrier account.

A safe purchase requires two separate “yes” answers: the exact phone supports eSIM, and it is permitted to use another provider’s network.
