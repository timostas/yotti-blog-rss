Brașov is easier to read in short autumn loops than as one long attractions list. Begin in Council Square, move past the Black Church and Catherine's Gate, then let the weather decide between the old walls and residential Șchei. A warm stop in the middle protects the evening even when a shower lingers.

The county's official tourism resource identifies the fortified old town and Șchei as defining parts of Brașov. They sit side by side but feel different: the square is open and formal; beyond the gate, the rhythm becomes domestic remarkably quickly.

## Give the square its quiet hour

Reach Piața Sfatului while the rain-darkened cobbles still have room around them. One circuit of the former council house is enough to locate the Black Church, Republicii Street and the passages that will bring you back later. Choose one interior rather than collecting entrances.

The Black Church remains an active place of worship, and its official site publishes current visiting hours and temporary closures. Check on the day. If the doors are closed, keep moving; the exterior remains part of the route, and the centre will bring you past again.

## From the gate to the old walls

Strada Sforii and Catherine's Gate lead towards the fortifications. Towers and stone walls sit beside schools, courtyards and everyday homes. Damp stone can be slippery, so shoes with grip are useful after rain.

In light rain, follow a short section of wall without turning the day into a climb on Tâmpa. The same principle shapes the [autumn vineyard walk in Vienna](https://yotti.net/en/blog/austria/vienna-vineyards-in-autumn-a-walk-from-nussdorf-to-kahlenberg): available light and the path underfoot matter more than collecting every marker.

## Walk through Șchei without treating it as a display

Șchei begins close to the gate yet has the character of a separate neighbourhood. Follow one residential street and notice doors, gradients and houses tucked against the hillside. Do not step into private courtyards for a photograph or stop in the narrowest part of the pavement.

## Make warmth part of the route

Use a café as a decision room, not just a reward at the finish. Take off the wet layer, look at the remaining light and choose another half-hour by the walls, a gentle pass through Șchei or an early return to the square. No food crawl is required.
