Your home number can remain available for calls and verification texts while a travel eSIM supplies mobile data. The safe configuration assigns voice and SMS to the home line, data to the trip line, and blocks automatic data fallback to the home SIM. The phone must support the relevant two-line combination.

Keeping a line switched on does not make its roaming free. Check the home carrier's terms for incoming calls and outgoing messages before departure. The separate [Yotti eSIM](https://yotti.net/en/catalog) covers data according to its own plan rather than changing those charges.

## Work out whether the two profiles can be active

A handset may store several eSIM profiles and still run only one of them at a time. If the home number uses a physical SIM, many dual-SIM phones can pair it with one eSIM. If the home number is already on eSIM, the device needs support for two active digital lines.

Apple supports Dual SIM on compatible iPhones and two active eSIMs on iPhone 13 models and later. Google exposes separate call, text and data choices on supported Pixel phones. Model, market variant and carrier support still matter, so confirm the exact device rather than relying on the number of saved plans.

## On iPhone, lock data to the trip line

Open Settings → Cellular after adding the plan. Give the two lines unmistakable labels such as “Home” and “Trip”. Under Cellular Data, select the travel eSIM.

Turn off Allow Cellular Data Switching when avoiding home-line data is the priority. Apple says that the switch can let the phone use another line for data during a call, subject to carrier support and availability. That flexibility can be useful at home and undesirable when the other line has roaming charges.

Keep the home number as the default voice line. An iPhone remembers the last line used for each contact, so check the label before an outbound call rather than assuming every contact follows the new default.

Enable data roaming on the travel eSIM if its setup instructions require it. Leave data roaming off for the home line. The home number can still register for calls and SMS; those services follow the home carrier's roaming agreement.

## Pixel and other Android phones separate the same jobs

On a Pixel, go to Settings → Network & internet → SIMs. Google provides individual defaults for calls, texts and mobile data. Choose the travel eSIM for data and the home SIM for calls and texts. “Ask every time” can help with outbound calls if the phone offers it.

Open each SIM's settings because roaming is controlled per line. Samsung, Xiaomi and other Android menus use different labels, but the check is identical: trip data should have no path to fall back to the home plan.

Pixel also has a setting for data during calls. Google notes that data can use the SIM handling the call. Leave that option off when a continuous connection matters less than preventing home-line roaming, unless the carrier terms make the use acceptable.

## Messages and calls stay attached to their numbers

A bank text goes to the number entered with the bank. Selecting a travel plan for data does not redirect that SMS. The home line must remain enabled and able to register with a partner network abroad.

Calls also retain the home carrier's pricing and limitations. If answering is expensive, an internet-calling app can use travel data, while the home line stays ready for important texts. Check voicemail and call forwarding before relying on them; both are carrier services.

iMessage, FaceTime and RCS combine account settings, phone numbers and internet access. Test them separately from carrier SMS after changing the data line. A working chat does not prove that an ordinary verification message can reach the home number.

## Verify the routing after landing

Open the SIM settings after landing and confirm that both named lines appear. Turn off Wi-Fi, load one lightweight page and inspect which plan records the data. Yotti's [installation page](https://yotti.net/en/how-it-works) shows the corresponding menus and the required roaming setting for its travel profile.

If the home line recorded data, switch its cellular data off immediately and save screenshots of the line selection and usage record. Do not erase either eSIM. Keeping the profiles and timestamps intact gives the home carrier enough context to investigate the session without creating a new installation problem.
