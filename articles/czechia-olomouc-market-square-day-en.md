Olomouc rewards a morning organised around one small taste rather than a monument inventory. Local tvarůžky supplies the sensory reference; Upper Square then shows how a market, a socialist-realist astronomical clock and a Baroque column occupy the same civic room.

## Order a sample, not a challenge

Tvarůžky is known for a forceful aroma and developed flavour. Ask for a small portion, its maturity and a sensible pairing before buying more. The exchange offers better context than treating the cheese as a dare.

## Read the clock before the performance

The Town Hall clock acquired its current appearance after wartime damage; VisitCzechia draws attention to its socialist-realist workers and professions. Study the mosaic before the usual viewing cluster forms.

<figure class="yotti-photo"><img src="https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/olo-clock.webp" srcset="https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/olo-clock-384w.webp 384w, https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/olo-clock-720w.webp 720w, https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/olo-clock-960w.webp 960w, https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/olo-clock.webp 1440w" sizes="(max-width: 760px) calc(100vw - 56px), 760px" width="1440" height="960" loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="A visitor studies the Olomouc astronomical clock"><figcaption>The facade reads as a post-war civic document, not a medieval survival.</figcaption></figure>

## Give the column enough distance

UNESCO describes the Holy Trinity Column as an outstanding expression of Moravian Baroque. Walk one exterior circuit before isolating a sculpture group. That order preserves both the monument’s silhouette and its relationship with the square.

<figure class="yotti-photo"><img src="https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/olo-column.webp" srcset="https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/olo-column-384w.webp 384w, https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/olo-column-720w.webp 720w, https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/olo-column-960w.webp 960w, https://timostas.github.io/yotti-blog-rss/assets/inline/olo-column.webp 1440w" sizes="(max-width: 760px) calc(100vw - 56px), 760px" width="1440" height="960" loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="The Holy Trinity Column on Olomouc Upper Square"><figcaption>A full circuit reveals civic scale before sculptural detail.</figcaption></figure>
