When the eSIM appears in settings but shows no carrier or signal bars, the profile has downloaded but the phone has not registered on a network. Confirm that the travel line is on, that you are inside the plan's destination area and that airplane mode is off. Do not erase the profile during these checks; its QR code may allow only one installation.

This is not the same as having signal with no working data. The immediate task is network registration. Check the countries and validity in the order or on the relevant [Yotti plan](https://yotti.net/en/).

## Confirm the correct line is enabled

On iPhone, open Settings, Cellular and the travel line, then turn on the line. On Pixel, open Network & internet and SIMs, then enable Use this SIM. Samsung places the equivalent control in SIM manager.

Read the line label rather than relying on its colour. A phone used for several trips may hold old profiles with similar partner names. Rename current plans by destination to avoid diagnosing the wrong one.

## Check the destination and validity

A regional eSIM does not have to register outside the countries listed in its package. Seeing no signal while still at home can be expected. After arrival, confirm that the current country is included and that the validity period has not ended.

A broad name such as “Europe” is not a definitive country list. Packages from different providers cover different territories. A connection may also be unavailable during a stopover even though it will work at the final destination.

## Ask the modem to search again

Turn airplane mode on for about thirty seconds, then turn it off. This restarts network search without changing the eSIM. Wait a few minutes before judging the result. Airports and border areas can expose several networks, and registration is not always immediate.

Restart the phone if the line remains blank. Recheck that the travel profile is enabled after boot. Change one setting at a time; simultaneous changes to network mode, operator selection and data settings make the cause impossible to identify.

## Leave network selection automatic first

Automatic selection is the normal starting point. If the plan instructions identify a specific local partner, open the network list and try that carrier manually. The scan itself may take several minutes.

“Forbidden” or a registration rejection usually points to plan authorisation rather than a weak signal. Return to automatic selection and give support the country, network name and exact error message.

## Separate coverage from profile status

An underground arrivals hall or a remote road can block every network. Move near a window or outdoors and see whether another phone on a local service has signal. If the handset can see local networks on its home SIM but the travel line cannot register, the plan status and destination coverage deserve closer attention.

Apple advises checking that both plans appear, that the carrier supports the service and that no account restriction is blocking the line. Android support may request the model, IMEI or EID. Share those identifiers only through an official support channel.

## Give support a useful report

Prepare the order number, country, exact phone model, time of the first attempt and screenshots of the line list and error. Do not include the full activation QR code. State that you have enabled the line, cycled airplane mode, restarted and returned network selection to automatic.

Once a carrier name and signal bars appear, stop changing networks.
