A journey from Yerevan to Dilijan gives mobile data two distinct jobs. Yerevan is the place to prove the line and correct settings while dependable Wi‑Fi is close. Before the road into Tavush, the priority shifts to an offline route, the accommodation address and transport contacts. A forest road is a poor place to discover that the new profile was never tested.

Before payment, confirm the exact phone model, carrier-lock status, allowance, validity and activation trigger. Install the profile at home. The separate guide to [installing an eSIM before travel](https://yotti.net/en/blog/how-to-install-an-esim-before-travel-without-starting-it-too-early) explains why downloading a profile and starting a plan may be different events.

## Prove the line in Yerevan

After arrival, assign the travel line to mobile data. Turn on data roaming for that line only when the plan instructions require it. Switch off Wi‑Fi, open two ordinary pages and send a message. A carrier name in the status bar does not prove that data is moving.

If the pages fail, check the selected data line, package status and roaming setting first. Do not delete the profile or change APN and network selection together. The [signal-but-no-data troubleshooting order](https://yotti.net/en/blog/esim-has-signal-but-no-internet-what-to-check) keeps every step reversible.

## Prepare before the road into Tavush

Armenia's official tourism portal describes the drive from Yerevan to Dilijan as roughly an hour and a half, passing part of Lake Sevan and the forested slopes of Tavush. Travel time varies, so use the transport provider's current route rather than a fixed timetable in an article.

Download the Dilijan area in an offline map. Save the first night's address in a form a driver can read, plus the ticket and accommodation phone number. Test every file in Airplane Mode. Keep the power bank in hand luggage.

## Use Dilijan data for decisions

At the stop, load the town map and message the host before walking away. For a forest trail, save the route beforehand and do not treat a coverage mark as a guarantee. Tell someone where you are going and when you expect to return. Connectivity supports ordinary trail preparation; it does not replace it.

Protect some allowance for navigation, messages and transport changes. Move photo backup, app updates and video to trusted Wi‑Fi. The guide to [choosing a travel data allowance](https://yotti.net/en/blog/how-much-data-do-you-need-for-a-travel-esim) helps separate essential use from media.
