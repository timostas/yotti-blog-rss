Two eSIMs can operate together only on phones that support two active digital lines. Other devices may store several profiles while allowing one eSIM to work alongside a physical SIM. The number of saved profiles and the number currently active are different specifications.

A common travel arrangement keeps a home number available for calls and verification messages while a [Yotti eSIM](https://yotti.net/en/) handles data. If the home number uses a physical SIM, this setup does not require dual active eSIM support.

## Dual eSIM on iPhone

Apple lists two active eSIMs for iPhone 13 and later and iPhone SE third generation. Regional editions can differ. Other compatible iPhones commonly combine one physical SIM with one eSIM.

An iPhone can store more profiles than it can use simultaneously. Active lines appear under Settings and Cellular, where the default data and voice lines are also selected.

## Two lines on Google Pixel

Google supports two active eSIM profiles on Pixel 7 and later when the carrier allows the feature. Pixel 3a and later models generally support dual-SIM arrangements, with regional and carrier exceptions among earlier devices.

Open Network & internet and SIMs to assign calls, messages and data. One line is normally the default for mobile data even while both remain available.

## Calls and messages have separate limits

Dual SIM Dual Standby keeps both lines listening while the phone is idle. Availability of the other line during a call depends on the model, carriers and Wi-Fi Calling. Apple and Google document different behaviour across device generations.

Many travel eSIMs are data-only, so assigning one to SMS does not create messaging service. A home number can remain enabled for bank codes, but the home carrier decides roaming charges for calls and texts.

## A controlled travel setup

Label the lines “Home” and “Travel”. Select Travel for mobile data and disable automatic switching if you want to prevent home-SIM roaming. Keep the home line as the voice default or ask the phone to prompt for each call.

Apply roaming to the travel line as instructed by the plan, while keeping home-line data roaming off. Disable Wi-Fi for a short test and check the data statistics to confirm which SIM carried the traffic.

## When the lines interfere

Voice-unavailable or interruption warnings can point to a network combination that does not work well together. Turn off one line and test each independently. If both work alone, support will need the phone model, software version and carrier names.

There is no need to erase either profile. A disabled eSIM remains stored for later use. Related line-selection guides are available under [Travel connectivity](https://yotti.net/en/blog/tag/Travel%20connectivity).
